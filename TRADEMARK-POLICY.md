# DuriOS Trademark & Attestation Naming Policy (`TRADEMARK-POLICY.md`)

- **Effective Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Status:** Draft Governance Policy (Pending formal trademark registration and external legal counsel review — see `plans/261017-open-core-hardening/plan.md`).

## 1. Relationship to Open-Source Copyright Licenses (`AGPL-3.0-only` & `MIT`)
**No Restriction on Copyright Permissions:** This Trademark Policy governs only the use of DuriOS brand identifiers and attestation seals to prevent public confusion regarding the origin of software builds or official inspection/attestation status. **Nothing in this policy restricts, modifies, or limits your copyright permissions under `AGPL-3.0-only` or `MIT`** (including your right to copy, modify, self-host across single or multiple sites, or operate modified versions over a network in compliance with AGPL-3.0 Section 13).

## 2. Covered Marks & Attestation Labels
Subject to formal registration, the following names, logos, and attestation labels identify the DuriOS project and its maintainers:
- Wordmarks: **"DuriOS"** and **"DuriOS Cloud"**
- Official Hosted Attestation Label: **"DuriOS Managed Cloud Attestation"**

*(Note: Local manifest verification outputs the purely technical status `"DuriOS Local Manifest Hash Check Passed (MIT Verifier)"`, which anyone may generate and share freely.)*

## 3. Nominative Fair Use (Permitted Without Separate Permission)
You may use the **"DuriOS"** name under standard nominative fair use principles without prior written permission when:
1. **Unmodified Self-Hosted Deployment:** Running unmodified releases of the Community Core on your own infrastructure (single-site or multi-site) and retaining the standard UI header indicating *"DuriOS Community Local Edition"*.
2. **Truthful Compatibility & Lineage Statements:** Stating truthfully that your software, fork, or service is *"based on DuriOS open-core"*, *"compatible with DuriOS `DossierExportManifest` JSON"*, or verified using the MIT-licensed `standalone-manifest-verifier`.

## 4. Prevention of Confusion Regarding Official Attestation
To protect growers, packhouses, customs brokers, and importers from misleading compliance or origin claims:
1. **Modified Forks & Third-Party Hosted Services:** If you distribute or operate a modified version or third-party hosted service under AGPL-3.0, you must clearly label it as an independent fork or third-party service so users are not misled into believing it is an official release or managed service operated by the DuriOS maintainers.
2. **No Misrepresentation of Official Attestation:** You may not represent local SHA-256 hashes, self-signed receipts, or third-party portal links as official DuriOS Cloud attestations or government/GACC customs approvals.
